One more step: set an encryption key

This key protects saved Xtream/M3U credentials at rest. Unlike the admin password, this can't be set through the app itself - it has to go in your environment config and the container needs a restart, since it's only ever read once at startup. Account creation and login stay disabled until this is done.

Copy this into docker-compose.yml, under this service's environment section, as ENCRYPTION_KEY=<value> - then restart the container.

Published quality data

streamcheck.pro publishes resolution, frame rate, codec and bitrate for whole IPTV providers. Naming yours means every channel shows its quality without being opened — and dead or blackscreen channels are flagged before you ever try one. Quality checks then read from it instead of measuring, and fall back to measuring for anything it does not list.

Searchable categories

Tick the categories worth searching and the rest stop appearing anywhere — channel search here, and the search in the watch portal. Channels you have already saved keep working whatever you pick. With nothing ticked, everything is searched.

Default categories for your networks

Tick the categories your channels live in and every broadcast and cable section below lists its channels from them, and auto-pick chooses from them. A section that names its own categories keeps those — open it and use Just for this one. The event sections are left alone: a provider files those listings per card, wherever it puts a one-off, so they go on searching the whole playlist. Searching is not affected either way — this adds nothing to Search categories and takes nothing away.

Link filters

Drops channels from every search, here and in the watch portal, using the published data for your provider. Counts are over your own channels, after the category filter. Leaving a whole row ticked means it is not filtering on that at all — and channels the sweep never covered are kept unless you say otherwise below.

Auto-pick channels

Tick a network and its channels are re-chosen from the published data every time your provider publishes a new sweep. Channels are matched by name and by playlist group, then ordered best-first: 1080p above 0.060 bpp, then 720p above 0.080, then the rest. Anything dead, blackscreen, unmeasured or under 60 fps is never picked — 30 fps only if a network has nothing else at all. A network that finds nothing keeps what it has.

Default presets

Pins the channels you have configured so they can be applied to another account in one click. Nothing is filled in automatically — press Apply on the preset you want. Stream ids only — no URLs and no provider credentials. Those ids are numbered by your IPTV provider, so a preset applies to that provider alone; save a separate one for each, and delete any that no longer apply.

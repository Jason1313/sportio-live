One more step: set an encryption key

This key protects saved Xtream/M3U credentials at rest. Unlike the admin password, this can't be set through the app itself - it has to go in your environment config and the container needs a restart, since it's only ever read once at startup. Account creation and login stay disabled until this is done.

Copy this into docker-compose.yml, under this service's environment section, as ENCRYPTION_KEY=<value> - then restart the container.

Network Links

When ESPN says an NFL or college football game is on one of these networks, Sportio shows your channels for that network instead of searching. Networks left empty fall back to category search, so nothing is ever missing entirely.
NFL RedZone is not attached to a game - it carries all of them at once, so ESPN never names it as anyone's broadcaster. Its channels appear as a permanent card at the top of the NFL row in the watch portal and in Stremio, every week, whether or not anything is on. Fill it in here and it plays; leave it empty and the card says so.
MMA works differently - the UFC channels below are shown in addition to search results, listed first. Up to 5 per section, tried in the order you set. Below them, Sportio adds any channel it finds by searching your Paramount+ PPV group for "UFC" - that is where providers put each card, under a name that changes every event, so there is nothing to configure for it here.
These channels are for UFC cards only. Dana White's Contender Series and PFL are found by their own automatic searches instead, because the UFC channels do not carry those shows. Everything else on the ESPN MMA schedule - RIZIN, LFA, UAE Warriors, Road to UFC - still gets a card, but no automatic search: there is no term that would be right across every promotion, and a wrong guess fills the list with channels showing another sport. Open the event in the watch portal and search your channels by hand for those.

Searchable categories

Tick the categories worth searching and the rest stop appearing anywhere — suggestions, channel search here, and the search in the watch portal. Channels you have already saved keep working whatever you pick. With nothing ticked, everything is searched.

Default presets

Pins the channels you have configured as the suggestions every new account starts from. Stream ids only — no URLs and no provider credentials. Those ids are numbered by your IPTV provider, so a preset applies to that provider alone; save a separate one for each, and delete any that no longer apply.

Settings

Xtream serves every channel two ways. If a stream opens your player but nothing ever plays, try the other one — providers often leave HLS switched off, and it fails silently rather than reporting an error.
